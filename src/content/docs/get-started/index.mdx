## Why {C.COMPANY}?

**{C.HYPERTABLE_CAP}s** automatically partition your data by time, making inserts and queries dramatically faster without changing your SQL.

**{C.HYPERCORE_CAP} storage engine** combines row and columnar storage—queries run up to 350x faster, ingestion is 44% faster, and storage shrinks by up to 98%.

**{C.CAGG_CAP}s** pre-compute and auto-refresh your analytics, so dashboards and reports respond instantly.

**Tiered storage** seamlessly moves cold data to low-cost object storage while keeping it fully queryable.

**100% {C.PG} compatible**—use your existing tools, drivers, and SQL skills.
