A Tiger Cloud service is a {C.PG} database instance extended by Tiger Data with custom capabilities. This means that any third-party solution that you can integrate with {C.PG}, you can also integrate with Tiger Cloud. See [the full list](https://slashdot.org/software/p/postgresql/integrations/) of {C.PG} integrations. Some of the most in-demand integrations are listed below.
