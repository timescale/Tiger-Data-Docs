[Azure Monitor](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/) is a comprehensive monitoring and observability service designed to help you maximize the availability and performance of your applications and services. It collects, analyzes, and acts on telemetry data from your cloud and on-premises environments.

You can export telemetry data from your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s with the time-series and analytics capability enabled to Azure Monitor. See [Exported metrics](/integrate/observability-alerting/exported-metrics) for the full list of default and additional metrics you can export.

This page explains how to export telemetry data from your {C.SERVICE_LONG} into Azure Monitor by creating a {C.CLOUD_LONG} data exporter, then attaching it to the {C.SERVICE_SHORT}. This integration is available for [Scale and Enterprise](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-azure/pricing-and-account-management#features-included-in-each-pricing-plan) pricing tiers.

<Prerequisites context="integration">
<PrereqsCloud />

- An Azure subscription with access to [Azure Monitor](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/) and [Application Insights](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/app/app-insights-overview).

  You need your [Azure Monitor Application Insights connection string](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/app/sdk-connection-string) to follow this procedure.
</Prerequisites>

## Create a data exporter

A {C.CLOUD_LONG} data exporter sends telemetry data from a {C.SERVICE_LONG} to third-party monitoring tools. You create an exporter on the [project level](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/security/members):

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, open Exporters`}></NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Click `New exporter`"></NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Select `Metrics` for `Data type` and `Azure Monitor` for provider">

<Image src={imgTigerConsoleIntegrationsAzureMonitor} alt="Adding an Azure Monitor exporter in Tiger Console" />

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Choose the region for your exporter"></NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Provide a name for your exporter">

The exporter name appears in {C.CONSOLE}, best practice is to make this name easily understandable.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Add your connection string">

[Learn where to find](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/app/create-workspace-resource?tabs=portal#get-the-connection-string) your connection string.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Export additional metrics">

Optionally tick `PostgreSQL metrics` to export [additional metrics](/integrate/observability-alerting/exported-metrics#postgresql-metrics), then click `Create exporter`.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

Your exporter is created and added to the list of already existing exporters.
