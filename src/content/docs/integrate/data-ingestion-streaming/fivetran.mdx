[Fivetran](https://fivetran.com/docs/getting-started) is a fully managed data pipeline platform that simplifies ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes
by automatically syncing data from multiple sources to your data warehouse.

<Image src={imgIntegrationsFivetranSyncData} alt="Fivetran synced data in a Tiger Cloud service" />

This page shows you how to inject data from data sources managed by Fivetran into a {C.SERVICE_LONG}.

<Prerequisites context="integration">
<IntegrationPrereqs />

- A [Fivetran](https://www.fivetran.com/) account.
</Prerequisites>

## Set your service as a destination in Fivetran

To be able to inject data into your {C.SERVICE_LONG}, set it as a destination in Fivetran:

<Image src={imgIntegrationsFivetranDestinationTimescalCloud} alt="Configuring Tiger Cloud as a Fivetran destination" />

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Add a destination">
    In [Fivetran Dashboard > Destinations](https://fivetran.com/dashboard/destinations), click `Add destination`.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title={`Select the ${C.PG} connector`}>
    Search for the {C.PG} connector and click `Select`. Add the destination name and click `Add`.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title={`Connect Fivetran to your ${C.SERVICE_SHORT}`}>
    In the {C.PG} setup, add your [{C.SERVICE_LONG} connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details), then click `Save & Test`.

    Fivetran validates the connection settings and sets up any security configurations.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Click `View Destination`">
    The `Destination Connection Details` page opens.
  </NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>

## Set up a Fivetran connection as your data source

In a real world scenario, you can select any of the over 600 connectors available in Fivetran to sync data with your
{C.SERVICE_LONG}. This section shows you how to inject the logs for your Fivetran connections into your {C.SERVICE_LONG}.

<Image src={imgIntegrationsFivetranDataSource} alt="Selecting a data source in Fivetran" />

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Add a connection">
    In [Fivetran Dashboard > Connections](https://fivetran.com/dashboard/connections), click `Add connection`.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Select the `Fivetran Platform` connector">
    Search for the `Fivetran Platform` connector, then click `Set up`.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Save and test the connection">
    Leave the default schema name, then click `Save & Test`. You see `All connection tests passed!`
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Build data models">
    Click `Continue`, select `Yes, build data models for me`, and click `Continue`. Your Fivetran connection is connected to your {C.SERVICE_LONG} destination.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Start the initial sync">
    Select `Start syncing all my data now` and click `Start initial sync`. Fivetran creates the log schema in your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and syncs the data to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
  </NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>

## View Fivetran data in your service

To see data injected by Fivetran into your {C.SERVICE_LONG}:

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Query the synced data">
    In {C.CONSOLE}, click `Data view`, select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, then run the following query:

    ```sql
    SELECT *
    FROM fivetran_log.account
    LIMIT 10;
    ```

    You see something like the following:

    <Image src={imgIntegrationsFivetranViewDataInService} alt="Viewing Fivetran-synced data in a Tiger Cloud service" />
  </NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>

You have successfully integrated Fivetran with {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
