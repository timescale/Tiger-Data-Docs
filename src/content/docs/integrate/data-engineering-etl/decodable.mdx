## Prerequisites

- Sign up for [Decodable](https://auth.decodable.co/u/signup/).

  This page uses the pipeline you create using the [Decodable Quickstart Guide](https://docs.decodable.co/get-started/quickstart.html).

## Connect Decodable to your service

To stream data gathered in Decodable to a {C.SERVICE_LONG}:

1. **Create the sync to pipe a Decodable data stream into your {C.SERVICE_LONG}**

   1. Log in to your [Decodable account](https://app.decodable.co/-/accounts).
   2. Click `Connections`, then click `New Connection`.
   3. Select a `PostgreSQL sink` connection type, then click `Connect`.
   4. Using your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details), fill in the connection information.

      Leave `schema` and `JDBC options` empty.
   5. Select the `http_events` source stream, then click `Next`.

      Decodable creates the table in your {C.SERVICE_LONG} and starts streaming data.

2. **Test the connection**

   1. Connect to your {C.SERVICE_LONG}.

      For {C.CLOUD_LONG}, open an [SQL editor](/build/data-management/run-queries-from-tiger-console) in [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/services). For {C.SELF_LONG}, use [`psql`](/integrate/query-administration/psql).

   2. Check the data from Decodable is streaming into your {C.SERVICE_LONG}.

      ```sql
      SELECT * FROM http_events;
      ```

      You see something like:

      <Image src={imgIntegrationsDecodableDataInService} alt="Decodable data visible in a Tiger Cloud service" />

You have successfully integrated Decodable with {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
