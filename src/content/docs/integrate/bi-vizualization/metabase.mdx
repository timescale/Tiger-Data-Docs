[Metabase](https://www.metabase.com/) is an open-source business intelligence tool that lets you ask questions of your data and share the answers in dashboards, without writing SQL.

This page shows you how to connect Metabase to {C.COMPANY} as a data source, so that you can explore and visualize the data in your service or database.

In this integration guide, you:

- Add your service or database to Metabase as a data source.
- Verify that Metabase can query your data.

<Prerequisites context="integration">
<IntegrationPrereqs />
<ConnectionDetails />
- A running [Metabase](https://www.metabase.com/start/) instance, either Metabase Cloud or a self-hosted installation.
</Prerequisites>

## Add your service or database to Metabase

Metabase connects to your service or database with standard {C.PG} connection details. To keep the connection read-only, you can optionally create a dedicated role with `SELECT` access before you start.

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Open the database settings in Metabase">

    In Metabase, go to **Settings** > **Admin settings** > **Databases**, then click `Add a database`.

  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Enter your connection details">

    From the **Database type** list, select `PostgreSQL`, then enter your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details):

    - **Host**: your service or database `host`.
    - **Port**: your `port`, usually `5432`.
    - **Database name**: your `dbname`.
    - **Username** and **Password**: your `user` and `password`.

    Give the connection a display name so you can recognize it later.

  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Configure connection options">

    {C.CLOUD_LONG} requires an encrypted connection, so turn on `Use a secure connection (SSL)`. If your service or database is only reachable through a bastion host, turn on `Use an SSH tunnel` and add the tunnel details.

    Optionally, restrict which schemas Metabase syncs, and set the `Database syncing` and `Scanning for filter values` schedules to control how often Metabase refreshes its view of your schema.

  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Save the connection">

    Click `Save`. Metabase connects to your service or database and starts an initial sync of your tables and columns.

    When the sync finishes, your tables appear in the Metabase data browser.

  </NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>

## Verify the integration

To confirm Metabase is working with your service or database:

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Ask a question">

    In Metabase, click `+ New` > `Question`, select your connection, then pick a table to query.

  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Visualize the results">

    Click `Visualize` to run the query.

    You see rows from your service or database rendered in the results area, confirming that Metabase can read your data.

  </NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>

You have successfully integrated Metabase with {C.COMPANY}.

## Troubleshooting

- **Connection times out or is refused:** confirm the host, port, and that your network allows connections from Metabase. For {C.CLOUD_LONG}, make sure SSL is enabled.
- **Tables don't appear after connecting:** trigger a manual sync from the database's settings in Metabase with `Sync database schema now`.

For other connectivity and authentication issues, see [Troubleshoot {C.CLOUD_LONG} integrations](/integrate/troubleshooting).
