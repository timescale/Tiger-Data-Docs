[Tableau](https://www.tableau.com/) is a popular analytics platform that helps you gain greater intelligence about your business. You can use it to visualize data stored in Tiger Cloud.

Prerequisites

To follow the steps on this page:

-   Create a target [Tiger Cloud service](/deploy/tiger-cloud/get-started/create-services) with Real-time analytics enabled.
    
    You need [your connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details). This procedure also works for [self-hosted TimescaleDB](/deploy/self-hosted/install-and-update/install-self-hosted).

-   Install [Tableau Server](https://www.tableau.com/support/releases/server/2024.2.6#esdalt) or sign up for [Tableau Cloud](https://www.tableau.com/products/trial).

Add your service as a virtual connection

To connect the data in your Tiger Cloud service to Tableau:

1.  **Log in to Tableau**
    -   Tableau Cloud: [sign in](http://online.tableau.com/), then click `Explore` and select a project.
    -   Tableau Desktop: sign in, then open a workbook.
2.  **Configure Tableau to connect to your Tiger Cloud service**
    1.  Add a new data source:
        -   Tableau Cloud: click `New` > `Virtual Connection`.
        -   Tableau Desktop: click `Data` > `New Data Source`.
    2.  Search for and select `PostgreSQL`. For Tableau Desktop download the driver and restart Tableau.
    3.  Configure the connection:
        -   `Server`, `Port`, `Database`, `Username`, `Password`: configure using your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details).
        -   `Require SSL`: tick the checkbox.
3.  **Click `Sign In` and connect Tableau to your service**

You have successfully integrated Tableau with Tiger Cloud.
