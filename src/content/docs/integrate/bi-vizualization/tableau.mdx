[Tableau](https://www.tableau.com/) is a popular analytics platform that helps you gain greater intelligence about your business. You can use it to visualize
data stored in {C.CLOUD_LONG}.

<Prerequisites context="integration">
<IntegrationPrereqs />

- A [Tableau Server](https://www.tableau.com/support/releases/server/2024.2.6#esdalt) installation or a [Tableau Cloud](https://www.tableau.com/products/trial) account.
</Prerequisites>

## Add your service as a virtual connection

To connect the data in your {C.SERVICE_LONG} to Tableau:

1. **Log in to Tableau**
   - Tableau Cloud: [sign in](http://online.tableau.com/), then click `Explore` and select a project.
   - Tableau Desktop: sign in, then open a workbook.

2. **Configure Tableau to connect to your {C.SERVICE_LONG}**
   1. Add a new data source:
      - Tableau Cloud: click `New` > `Virtual Connection`.
      - Tableau Desktop: click `Data` > `New Data Source`.
   2. Search for and select `PostgreSQL`.

      For Tableau Desktop download the driver and restart Tableau.
   3. Configure the connection:
      - `Server`, `Port`, `Database`, `Username`, `Password`: configure using your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details).
      - `Require SSL`: tick the checkbox.

3. **Click `Sign In` and connect Tableau to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}**

You have successfully integrated Tableau with {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
