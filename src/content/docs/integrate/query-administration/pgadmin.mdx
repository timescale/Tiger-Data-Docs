[pgAdmin](https://www.pgadmin.org/) is a feature-rich open-source administration and development platform for {C.PG}. It is available for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and
Safari browsers, or can be installed on Microsoft Windows, Apple macOS, or various Linux flavors.

<Image src={imgTimescaleCloudPgadmin} alt="pgAdmin connected to a Tiger Cloud service" />

This page explains how to integrate pgAdmin with your {C.SERVICE_LONG}.

<Prerequisites context="integration">
<IntegrationPrereqs />
<ConnectionDetails />

- [pgAdmin](https://www.pgadmin.org/download/) downloaded and installed.
</Prerequisites>

## Connect pgAdmin to your service

To connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}:

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Start pgAdmin" />
  <NumberedItem title="In the `Quick Links` section of the `Dashboard` tab, click `Add New Server`" />
  <NumberedItem title="In `Register - Server` > `General`, fill in the `Name` and `Comments` fields with the server name and description, respectively" />
  <NumberedItem title="Configure the connection">
    1. In the `Connection` tab, configure the connection using your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details).
    1. If you configured your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to connect using a [stricter SSL mode](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/security/strict-ssl), then in the `SSL` tab check `Use SSL`, set `SSL mode` to the configured mode, and in the `CA Certificate` field type the location of the SSL root CA certificate to use.
  </NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Click `Save`" />
</NumberedList>

You have successfully integrated pgAdmin with {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
