[Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/) is a suite of cloud computing services, offering scalable infrastructure, AI, analytics, databases, security, and developer tools to help businesses build, deploy, and manage applications.

This page explains how to integrate your Google Cloud infrastructure with Tiger Cloud using [AWS Transit Gateway](https://aws.amazon.com/transit-gateway/).

## Prerequisites

To follow the steps on this page:

- Create a target [Tiger Cloud service](/deploy/tiger-cloud/get-started/create-services) with the Real-time analytics capability. You need your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details).
- Set up [AWS Transit Gateway](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/vpc/latest/tgw/tgw-getting-started.html).
- Establish connectivity between Google Cloud and AWS. See [Connect HA VPN to AWS peer gateways](https://cloud.google.com/network-connectivity/docs/vpn/how-to/connect-ha-vpn-aws-peer-gateway).

<Callout variant="note">
  This feature is currently not supported for Tiger Cloud on Microsoft Azure.
</Callout>

## Connect your Google Cloud infrastructure to your Tiger Cloud services

To connect to Tiger Cloud:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Connect your infrastructure to AWS Transit Gateway">

Establish connectivity between Google Cloud and AWS. See [Connect HA VPN to AWS peer gateways](https://cloud.google.com/network-connectivity/docs/vpn/how-to/connect-ha-vpn-aws-peer-gateway).

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a Peering VPC in Tiger Console">

<ol type="a">
  <li>In [Tiger Console](https://console.cloud.timescale.com/), go to `Security` &gt; `VPC`, then click `Create a VPC` (see screenshot below).</li>
  <li>Choose your region and IP range, name your VPC, then click `Create VPC`. Your service and Peering VPC must be in the same AWS region. The number of Peering VPCs you can create in your project depends on your [pricing plan](/deploy/tiger-cloud/understand/pricing-and-account-management). If you need another Peering VPC, either contact [support@tigerdata.com](mailto:support@tigerdata.com) or change your plan in [Tiger Console](https://console.cloud.timescale.com/).</li>
  <li>Add a peering connection: in the `VPC Peering` column click `Add`; provide your AWS account ID, Transit Gateway ID, CIDR ranges, and AWS region (Tiger Cloud creates a new isolated connection for every unique Transit Gateway ID); then click `Add connection` (see screenshot below).</li>
</ol>

![Tiger Cloud new VPC](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/add-peering-vpc-tiger-cloud.png)

![Create a new VPC in Tiger Cloud](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/configure-peering-vpc-tiger-cloud.png)

![Add peering](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/add-peering-tiger-cloud.png)

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Accept and configure peering connection in your AWS account">

Once your peering connection appears as `Processing`, you can accept and configure it in AWS:

<ol type="a">
  <li>Accept the peering request coming from Tiger Cloud. The request can take up to 5 min to arrive. Within 5 more minutes after accepting, the peering should appear as `Connected` in Tiger Console.</li>
  <li>Configure at least the following in your AWS account networking: your subnet route table to route traffic to your Transit Gateway for the Peering VPC CIDRs; your Transit Gateway route table to route traffic to the newly created Transit Gateway peering attachment for the Peering VPC CIDRs; and security groups to allow outbound TCP 5432.</li>
</ol>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Attach a Tiger Cloud service to the Peering VPC in Tiger Console">

<ol type="a">
  <li>In [Tiger Console](https://console.cloud.timescale.com/dashboard/services), select the service you want to connect to the Peering VPC.</li>
  <li>Click `Operations` &gt; `Security` &gt; `VPC`.</li>
  <li>Select the VPC, then click `Attach VPC`.</li>
</ol>

<Callout variant="warning">
  You cannot attach a Tiger Cloud service to multiple Tiger Cloud VPCs at the same time.
</Callout>

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

You have successfully integrated your Google Cloud infrastructure with Tiger Cloud.
