For full instance physical backups (which are especially useful for starting up
new [replicas](/deploy/self-hosted/replication-and-ha)), [`pg_basebackup`](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/app-pgbasebackup.html)
works with all {C.TIMESCALE_DB} installation types. You can also use any of several
external backup and restore managers such as [`pg_backrest`](https://pgbackrest.org/), or [`barman`](https://pgbarman.org/). These tools all allow
you to take online, physical backups of your entire instance, and many offer
incremental backups and other automation options.
