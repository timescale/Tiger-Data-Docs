{C.TIMESCALE_DB} takes advantage of the reliable backup and restore functionality
provided by {C.PG}. There are a few different mechanisms you can use to
back up your {C.SELF_LONG} database:

*   [Logical backup](/deploy/self-hosted/backup-and-restore/logical-backup) with `pg_dump` and `pg_restore`.
*   [Physical backup](/deploy/self-hosted/backup-and-restore/physical) with `pg_basebackup` or another tool.
