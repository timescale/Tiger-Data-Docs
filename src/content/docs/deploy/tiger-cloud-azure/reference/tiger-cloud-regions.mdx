## Overview

Tiger Cloud services are deployed across multiple geographic regions to provide low-latency access, data residency compliance, and disaster recovery options. This reference guide lists all available regions and provides guidance on region selection.

## Prerequisites

- A Tiger Cloud account
- Understanding of your application's geographic requirements
- Knowledge of any data residency or compliance requirements

## Choosing a region

Factors to consider when selecting a region for your Tiger Cloud service.

### Latency considerations

How to minimize latency for your users:
- Deploy in the region closest to your primary user base
- Use multiple regions for globally distributed applications
- Consider read replicas in secondary regions

### Data residency and compliance

Regulatory considerations for region selection:
- GDPR requirements for EU data
- Data sovereignty laws
- Industry-specific compliance (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, etc.)

### Service availability

Understanding availability zones within regions:
- Each region contains multiple isolated availability zones
- High availability deployments span multiple zones
- Zone failures do not affect other zones in the region

### Pricing differences

Regional pricing variations:
- Compute and storage costs may vary by region
- Data transfer costs between regions
- Consider total cost of ownership

## Multi-region deployments

Options for deploying across multiple regions.

### Read replicas across regions

Using cross-region replicas for:
- Reduced read latency for global users

Setting up multi-region failover for business continuity.

Common issues and solutions:
