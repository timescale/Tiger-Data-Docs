A support bundle collects the logs, metrics, and configuration the {C.COMPANY} team needs to
diagnose an issue. Generate one before you contact support.

## Generate a bundle

Generate a support bundle from the support area of the console.

## Share it

Share the generated bundle with the {C.COMPANY} support team along with a description of the issue.
