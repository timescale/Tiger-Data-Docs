## A deployment fails

Check the Operations tab in the console, which streams each step of the deployment. Download the
logs to see the underlying error.

## Dashboards or health cards are empty

A required port is usually blocked between the console and the host. Confirm that ports `8008`,
`9100`, `9187`, `9399`, and `9127` are open. See
[system requirements](/get-started/enterprise/requirements) for what each port is for.

## The console cannot reach a host

Confirm that the console's public SSH key is authorized on the host, that the SSH user is correct,
and that port `22` is open.

## Collect diagnostics

Generate a [support bundle](/deploy/enterprise/support) and share it with the {C.COMPANY}
support team.
