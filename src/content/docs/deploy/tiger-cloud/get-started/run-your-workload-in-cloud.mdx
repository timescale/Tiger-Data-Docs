## Overview

This guide walks you through deploying and running your database workload in Tiger Cloud. Whether you are migrating an existing PostgreSQL database or starting fresh, you will learn how to set up your service, migrate data, and monitor performance.

## Prerequisites

- A Tiger Cloud account with an active subscription
- Connection details for your source database (if migrating)
- Understanding of your workload requirements (storage, compute, throughput)
- Application connection strings ready to update

## Plan your deployment

Key decisions to make before deploying your workload.

### Choose the right service size

Selecting compute and storage resources:
- CPU and memory requirements
- Expected data volume and growth rate
- Concurrent connection needs
- Read vs. write workload balance

### Select your region

Factors for region selection:
- Proximity to your application servers
- Data residency requirements
- Availability zone considerations

### Configure high availability

Deciding on replica configuration:
- Development vs. production requirements
- High performance vs. high data integrity modes
- Cost considerations
