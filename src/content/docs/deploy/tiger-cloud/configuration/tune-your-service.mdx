## Overview

Tiger Cloud provides sensible defaults for PostgreSQL configuration, but optimizing settings for your specific workload can significantly improve performance. This guide covers the key parameters you can tune and best practices for different workload types.

## Prerequisites

- An active Tiger Cloud service
- Understanding of your workload characteristics (read-heavy, write-heavy, mixed)
- Access to service configuration settings

## Understanding Tiger Cloud defaults

Why default settings may not be optimal for your workload:
- Defaults are conservative for broad compatibility
- Your specific workload may benefit from tuning
- Resource utilization can be improved with proper configuration
