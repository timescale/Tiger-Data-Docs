AWS PrivateLink creates a private endpoint in your {C.VPC} that routes traffic to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} over the AWS backbone, without crossing the public internet or requiring {C.VPC} peering. This page describes how to authorize your AWS account in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}, create the VPC endpoint on the AWS side, and attach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to the resulting PrivateLink connection.

## Prerequisites

<PrereqsCloud />

* Create an [AWS account](https://signin.aws.amazon.com/signup?request_type=register) with a [VPC](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/vpc/latest/userguide/create-vpc.html) and a subnet for the resources you will connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}.

## Set up AWS PrivateLink connection

<Callout variant="note">

AWS PrivateLink is currently in private preview. To request access in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints` and click `Request access`. Then refresh the page and follow the steps below.

</Callout>

Take the following steps to connect {C.CLOUD_LONG} to AWS with PrivateLink:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Create an AWS account authorization">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/aws-privatelink), select `Security` > `Private Endpoints` > `Configure Private Endpoint Connection`.

   <Image src={imgPrivateEndpointTigerCloud} alt="Configure Private Endpoint Connection in Tiger Cloud" />

1. In `Principal ID`, enter your AWS account ID and give your connection a name, for convenience.

   <Callout variant="warning">

   Click the checkmark next to the connection name to save it. Otherwise, your input is discarded.

   </Callout>

1. Under `Service name`, copy the alias for the region in which you need to create the connection. Choose the region of your AWS resources.
1. Click `Done`. {C.CLOUD_LONG} confirms your authorization. Once it is confirmed, you can create multiple endpoints from the same authorized account.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a VPC endpoint in AWS">

1. In AWS Console, go to `VPC` > `Endpoints` > `Create endpoint`.
1. Optionally provide a name tag for your endpoint.
1. Under `Type`, select `PrivateLink Ready partner services`.

   <Image src={imgAwsPrivateEndpointSettings} alt="AWS Create endpoint settings" />

1. Under `Service settings` > `Service name`, paste in the alias your copied in {C.CONSOLE} and click `Verify service`. Wait for the `Service name verified` success message.

   <Image src={imgAwsPrivateEndpointServiceSettings} alt="AWS endpoint Service settings" />

1. Under `Network settings`, select the VPC with the resources that you want connected to {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
1. Under `Subnets`, select the Availability Zone and subnet.
1. Optionally configure the security groups and tags, then click `Create endpoint`.
1. Go to `EC2` > `Network interfaces` and copy the primary private IPv4 address of your endpoint.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Sync the connection">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/aws-privatelink) > `Security` > `Private Endpoints`, click `Refresh`. {C.CLOUD_LONG} automatically approves connections from authorized accounts. Your connection appears in the list.

  <Image src={imgPrivateEndpointConnectionAuthorized} alt="Authorized Private Endpoint connection in Tiger Cloud" />

1. Click `Add IP` and paste the VPC endpoint IP address you have copied from AWS.
1. Click the three dots next to your connection and select `+ Attach service`. Select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from the dropdown and click `Attach`. You can attach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to one PrivateLink connection.
1. From an EC2 instance inside your VPC, connect to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} using a connection string with your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details). You should be able to connect successfully.

<Callout variant="warning">

After attaching a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to a PrivateLink connection, it is no longer accessible from the public internet. Make sure all your applications are configured to connect through the VPC endpoint before attaching your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.

</Callout>

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Manage connections

- To detach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from a PrivateLink connection, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints` and expand the details of the corresponding connection. Click the trash bin icon and confirm detaching your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
- To edit or remove a connection, click the three dots next to the connection in the list and select `Edit` or `Disconnect`, respectively. Detach {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s from the connection before deleting it.
- To remove an authorization, click `Manage Authorizations` > trash bin icon. Disconnect all relevant connections before removing an authorization.
