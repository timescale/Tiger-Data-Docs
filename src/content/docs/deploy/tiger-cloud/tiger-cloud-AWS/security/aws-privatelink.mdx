With {C.CLOUD_LONG}, you can connect your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s to AWS PrivateLink endpoints. A private endpoint in your {C.VPC} routes traffic to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} over the AWS backbone, without crossing the public internet or requiring {C.VPC} peering. This page describes how to create a private endpoint on the AWS side, claim it in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}, and attach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to it.

<Prerequisites context="procedure">
<PrereqsCloud />

* An [AWS account](https://signin.aws.amazon.com/signup?request_type=register) with a [VPC](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/vpc/latest/userguide/create-vpc.html) and a subnet for the resources you will connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
* [IAM permissions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/vpc/latest/privatelink/identity-access-management.html) configured to create VPC endpoints.
</Prerequisites>

## Set up a private endpoint connection

Take the following steps to connect your {C.SERVICE_LONG} to a PrivateLink endpoint.

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Copy the alias for your region">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/aws-privatelink), select `Security` > `Private Endpoints` > `Manage endpoints`.
1. Under `Create the endpoint`, copy the `Alias` for the region in which you need to create the connection. Choose the region of your AWS resources.

   <Callout variant="warning">

   Screenshot pending: the `Manage endpoints` panel.

   </Callout>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a VPC endpoint in AWS">

1. In [AWS Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/), go to `VPC` > `Endpoints` > `Create endpoint`.
1. Optionally provide a name tag for your endpoint.
1. Under `Type`, select `PrivateLink Ready partner services`.

   <Image src={imgAwsPrivateEndpointSettings} alt="AWS create endpoint settings" />

1. Under `Service settings` > `Service name`, paste in the alias your copied in {C.CONSOLE} and click `Verify service`. Wait for the `Service name verified.` success message.

   <Image src={imgAwsPrivateEndpointServiceSettings} alt="AWS endpoint service settings" />

1. Select the VPC that contains the resources you want to connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}, then choose one or more Availability Zones and subnets where the endpoint's private IPs will be created. For lowest latency and no cross-AZ data transfer charges, match the Availability Zones of your workloads; select multiple AZs for high availability.
1. Optionally configure the security groups and tags, then click `Create endpoint`.
1. Copy the VPC endpoint ID of your new endpoint, in the form `vpce-0a78876addf83f367`. You claim the connection with this ID.
1. Go to `EC2` > `Network interfaces` and copy the primary private IPv4 address of your endpoint.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Claim the connection">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/aws-privatelink) > `Security` > `Private Endpoints`, click `Manage endpoints`.
1. Under `Claim it`, paste your VPC endpoint ID into `Endpoint identifier`, then click `Claim connection`. {C.CLOUD_LONG} links the connection to the project you are working in, and it appears in the list.

   <Callout variant="warning">

   Screenshot pending: the `Claim it` section of the `Manage endpoints` panel.

   </Callout>

   <Callout variant="note">

   You can claim a VPC endpoint against one project only.

   Your VPC endpoint ID can also take a minute to become claimable after you create the endpoint in AWS.

   </Callout>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Configure and attach">

1. Under `IP Address` click `Add IP` and paste the private endpoint IP address you have copied from AWS.
1. Under `Services` click `Attach service`. Select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from the dropdown and click `Attach`. You can attach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to one private endpoint.
1. From an EC2 instance inside your VPC, connect to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} using a connection string with your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details). You should be able to connect successfully.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Manage connections

- To detach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from a private endpoint connection, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints`, expand the arrow in the `Services` column, and click the trash icon next to the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} connection string.
- To edit or remove an endpoint connection, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints` and click the three dots next to the connection in the list. Select `Edit` or `Disconnect`, respectively. You need to detach all {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s from a private endpoint connection before deleting it.
