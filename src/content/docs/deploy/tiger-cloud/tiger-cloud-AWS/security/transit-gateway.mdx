[AWS Transit Gateway](https://aws.amazon.com/transit-gateway/) enables you to securely connect to your **{C.CLOUD_LONG} on AWS** from Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, AWS, or any other cloud or on-premise environment.

You use AWS Transit Gateway as a traffic controller for your network. Instead of setting up multiple direct connections to different clouds, on-premise data centers, and other AWS services, you connect everything to AWS Transit Gateway. This simplifies your network and makes it easier to manage and scale.

You can then create a peering connection between your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s and AWS Transit Gateway in {C.CLOUD_LONG}. This means that, no matter how big or complex your infrastructure is, you can connect securely to your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s.

For enhanced security, you can add peering connections to multiple Transit Gateways with overlapping CIDRs—{C.CLOUD_LONG} creates a new isolated connection for every unique Transit Gateway ID. Otherwise, the existing connection is reused for your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s in the same project and region.

To configure this secure connection, you:

1. Connect your infrastructure to AWS Transit Gateway.
1. Create a {C.CLOUD_LONG} Peering {C.VPC} with a peering connection to AWS Transit Gateway.
1. Accept and configure the peering connection on your side.
1. Attach individual {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s to the Peering {C.VPC}.

AWS Transit Gateway enables you to connect from almost any environment, this page provides examples for the most common use cases.

<TabItem label="Amazon Web Services">

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Create a Peering ${C.VPC} in ${C.CONSOLE}`}>

<ol type="a">
<li>In <code>Security</code> &gt; <code>VPC</code>, click <code>Create a VPC</code>: <img src="https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/add-peering-vpc-tiger-console.png" alt={`${C.CLOUD_LONG} new ${C.VPC}`} /></li>
<li>Choose your region and IP range, name your VPC, then click <code>Create VPC</code>: <img src="https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/configure-peering-vpc-tiger-console.png" alt={`Create a new VPC in ${C.CLOUD_LONG}`} /> Your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and Peering {C.VPC} must be in the same AWS region. The number of Peering {C.VPC}s you can create in your project depends on your <a href="/TODO/pricing-and-account-management/">{C.PRICING_PLAN}</a>. If you need another Peering {C.VPC}, either contact <a href="mailto:support@tigerdata.com">support@tigerdata.com</a> or change your plan in <a href="https://console.cloud.timescale.com/">{C.CONSOLE}</a>.</li>
<li>In the <code>VPC Peering</code> column, click <code>Add</code>.</li>
<li>Provide your AWS account ID, Transit Gateway ID, CIDR ranges, and AWS region. {C.CLOUD_LONG} creates a new isolated connection for every unique Transit Gateway ID. <img src="https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/add-peering-tiger-console.png" alt="Add peering" /></li>
<li>Click <code>Add connection</code>.</li>
</ol>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Accept and configure peering connection in your AWS account">

Once your peering connection appears as `Processing`, you can accept and configure it in AWS:

<ol type="a">
<li>Accept the peering request coming from {C.CLOUD_LONG}. The request can take up to 5 min to arrive. Within 5 more minutes after accepting, the peering should appear as <code>Connected</code> in {C.CONSOLE}.</li>
<li>Configure at least the following in your AWS account networking: your subnet route table to route traffic to your Transit Gateway for the Peering VPC CIDRs; your Transit Gateway route table to route traffic to the newly created Transit Gateway peering attachment for the Peering VPC CIDRs; and security groups to allow outbound TCP 5432.</li>
</ol>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`Attach a ${C.CLOUD_LONG} service to the Peering VPC in ${C.CONSOLE}`}>

<ol type="a">
<li>Select the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} you want to connect to the Peering VPC.</li>
<li>Click <code>Operations</code> &gt; <code>Security</code> &gt; <code>VPC</code>.</li>
<li>Select the VPC, then click <code>Attach VPC</code>.</li>
</ol>

You cannot attach a {C.SERVICE_LONG} to multiple {C.CLOUD_LONG} {C.VPC}s at the same time.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

</TabItem>
