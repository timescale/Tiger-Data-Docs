{C.CLOUD_LONG} provides comprehensive backup and recovery solutions to protect your data, including automatic daily backups and point-in-time recovery.

## Automatic backups

{C.CLOUD_LONG} automatically handles backup for your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s using the `pgBackRest` tool. You don't need to perform
backups manually.

{C.CLOUD_LONG} automatically creates one full backup every week, and incremental backups every day in the same region as
your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}. Additionally, all [Write-Ahead Log (WAL)](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/wal-intro.html) files are retained back to the oldest full backup.
This means that you always have a full backup available for the current and previous week:

![Backup in Tiger Cloud](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/database-backup-recovery.png)

On [{C.SCALE} and {C.PERFORMANCE}](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-azure/pricing-and-account-management) {C.PRICING_PLAN}s, you can check the list of backups for the previous 14 days in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}. To do so, select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, then click `Operations` > `Backup and restore` > `Backup history`.

In the event of a storage failure, a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} automatically recovers from a backup
to the point of failure. If the whole availability zone goes down, your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s are recovered in a different zone. In the event of a user error, you can [create a point-in-time recovery fork](#create-a-point-in-time-recovery-fork).

## Create a point-in-time recovery fork

<PitrIntro />

You initiate a point-in-time recovery in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Ensure the ${C.SERVICE_SHORT} is running or paused`}>

In [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/services), from the `Services` list, ensure the {C.SERVICE_SHORT}
you want to recover has a status of `Running` or `Paused`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a recovery fork">

Navigate to `Operations` > `Backup & restore` and click `Create recovery fork`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Select the recovery point">

Select the recovery point, ensuring the correct time zone (UTC offset).

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Configure the fork">

![Create recovery fork](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/create-recovery-fork-tiger-console.png)

You can configure the compute resources, add an HA replica, tag your fork, and
add a connection pooler. Best practice is to match
the same configuration you had at the point you want to recover to.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Confirm the recovery fork">

Confirm by clicking `Create recovery fork`.

A fork of the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} is created. The recovered {C.SERVICE_SHORT} shows in `Services` with a label specifying which {C.SERVICE_SHORT} it has been forked from.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Update the connection strings in your app">

Update the connection strings in your app to use the fork.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>
