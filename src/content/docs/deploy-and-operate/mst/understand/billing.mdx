## Overview

Managed Service for TimescaleDB (MST) uses a transparent, usage-based pricing model. You pay for the compute, storage, and network resources your database consumes, with no hidden fees or long-term commitments required.

## Pricing components

Your MST bill consists of several components based on resource consumption.

### Compute

Compute charges are based on the instance type and running time.

- **vCPU hours** - Charged per hour based on the number of virtual CPUs provisioned
- **Memory** - Included with compute tier selection
- **Instance types** - Various configurations optimized for different workloads

### Storage

Storage charges vary by tier and usage.

- **High-performance storage** - SSD-backed storage for active data, charged per GB/month
- **Object storage tier** - Low-cost S3-based storage for historical data, charged per GB/month
- **Backup storage** - Automated backups and point-in-time recovery snapshots

### Network

Data transfer charges for traffic in and out of your database.

- **Data ingress** - Typically free for data coming into the database
- **Data egress** - Charged per GB for data transferred out of the cloud region
- **Cross-region traffic** - Additional charges for data transferred between regions

## Pricing models

Choose the pricing model that best fits your usage patterns.

### On-demand pricing

Pay-as-you-go pricing with no upfront commitment.

- Billed hourly for compute, monthly for storage
- Scale up or down at any time
- Best for variable or unpredictable workloads
- No minimum commitment

### Committed use discounts

Reserve capacity for one or three years for significant savings.

- Up to 30-60% discount compared to on-demand
- Applies to compute resources (CPU and memory)
- Storage remains pay-as-you-go
- Best for stable, predictable workloads

## Understanding your bill

How to read and interpret your MST billing statements.

### Billing cycle

Bills are generated monthly, with charges calculated based on:
- Hourly compute usage aggregated for the month
- Peak storage consumption during the billing period
- Total network egress for the period

### Cost breakdown

Where to find detailed cost breakdowns by:
- Service or instance
- Resource type (compute, storage, network)
- Time period
- Project or cost center tags

## Cost optimization

Strategies to reduce your MST costs without sacrificing performance.

### Right-sizing instances

Match instance size to actual workload requirements. Monitor CPU and memory utilization to identify over-provisioned resources.

### Using tiered storage

Move infrequently accessed historical data to the low-cost object storage tier. The cost difference between tiers can be 10x or more.

### Compression

Enable TimescaleDB compression to reduce storage consumption by up to 90%, directly lowering storage costs.

### Data retention policies

Implement automated retention policies to delete data that is no longer needed, rather than paying to store it indefinitely.

### Reserved capacity

For stable workloads, committed use discounts provide substantial savings over on-demand pricing.
