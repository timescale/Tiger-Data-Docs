TimescaleDB Enterprise is a commercially licensed distribution of {C.TIMESCALE_DB} that you run in
your own self-managed environment: on-premises, at the edge, or in a private cloud. It runs the same
{C.PG} and {C.TIMESCALE_DB} engine as {C.TDB_COMMUNITY}, with production operations and commercial
support included. {C.HYPERTABLE}s, {C.HYPERCORE}, {C.CAGG}s, and {C.HYPERFUNC} work on infrastructure
you control.

## Who it's for

TimescaleDB Enterprise is for teams that keep their database on infrastructure they operate
themselves, for reasons that a managed cloud cannot satisfy:

- **Security, compliance, and data sovereignty**: data must stay within a specific network, region,
  or facility, or fully air-gapped.
- **Existing self-hosters**: teams already running {C.TDB_COMMUNITY} who want high availability,
  backups, upgrades, monitoring, and vendor support without building and maintaining that tooling
  themselves.

If you can run in a managed cloud, {C.CLOUD_LONG} handles operations for you. Choose TimescaleDB
Enterprise when your database has to stay on infrastructure you operate.

## One console for everything

The console is the command center for your deployment. From it you register infrastructure hosts,
deploy and manage clusters, run backups and restores, monitor cluster health, and coordinate
upgrades. Because it runs on infrastructure you provide, everything you do stays within your
environment.

## How it works

You run the console as a container-based stack on a host you provide. It connects over SSH to one or
more Linux hosts that you register and provisions {C.TIMESCALE_DB} on them. One host can serve as a
dedicated backup target.
