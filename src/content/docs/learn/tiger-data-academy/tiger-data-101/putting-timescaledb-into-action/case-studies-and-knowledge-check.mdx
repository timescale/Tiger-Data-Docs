## Real-world case studies

### ControlCom: 300+ million monthly data points

Facility management company processes 300M+ data points monthly from HVAC and energy sensors.

**Challenge:** Manage cost and performance with massive data volumes.

**Solution:**
- hypertables with daily chunks
- hypercore compression after 7 days (15x reduction)
- Tiering to S3 after 30 days
- Hierarchical continuous aggregates for multi-resolution dashboards

**Result:** 90% storage cost reduction, real-time and historical analytics in one system.
