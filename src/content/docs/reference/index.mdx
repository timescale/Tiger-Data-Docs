## {C.TIMESCALE_DB}

SQL functions, views, and settings for {C.TIMESCALE_DB}, on both {C.CLOUD_LONG} and {C.SELF_LONG}. See the [{C.TIMESCALE_DB} reference](/reference/timescaledb) for:

- [Hypertables and chunks](/reference/timescaledb/hypertables): create and manage {C.HYPERTABLE}s and {C.CHUNK}s, including size and statistics, tablespaces, reordering, and query optimization.
- [Hypercore](/reference/timescaledb/hypercore): the {C.HYPERCORE} columnstore, including columnstore policies, manual conversion, and per-{C.CHUNK} settings.
- [Continuous aggregates](/reference/timescaledb/continuous-aggregates): create, modify, and schedule refresh policies for {C.CAGG}s.
- [Hyperfunctions](/reference/timescaledb/hyperfunctions): time-series utilities, distribution analysis, and gapfilling.
- [Data retention](/reference/timescaledb/data-retention): drop old data on a schedule.
- [Jobs and automation](/reference/timescaledb/jobs-automation): schedule and manage user-defined actions.
- [UUIDv7 functions](/reference/timescaledb/uuid-functions): generate and inspect time-ordered UUIDs.
- [Informational views](/reference/timescaledb/informational-views): inspect {C.HYPERTABLE}s, {C.CHUNK}s, columnstore settings, jobs, and policies.
- [Configuration](/reference/timescaledb/configuration): GUCs and {C.PG} configuration parameters.
- [Administration](/reference/timescaledb/administration): administrative functions and commands.
