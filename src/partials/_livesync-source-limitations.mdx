<Callout variant="warning" title="Source chunk operations">
During the migration or sync:

- On a {C.TIMESCALE_DB} source, do not [merge](/reference/timescaledb/hypertables/merge_chunks) or [split](/reference/timescaledb/hypertables/split_chunk) {C.CHUNK}s.
- On a {C.CLOUD_LONG} source, do not run tiering policies or manually [tier](/reference/tiger-cloud/data-tiering/tier_chunk) or [untier](/reference/tiger-cloud/data-tiering/untier_chunk) {C.CHUNK}s.

If the source is a {C.CLOUD_LONG} service, disable its tiering policies before you start.
Use [`alter_job`](/reference/timescaledb/jobs-automation/alter_job) with `scheduled => false` for each tiering policy job.
The target can keep its tiering policies enabled.
</Callout>
