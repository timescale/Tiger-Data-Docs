import * as C from "@constants";

1. **Create a Peering {C.VPC} in [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.timescale.com/)**

   1. In `Security` > `VPC`, click `Create a VPC`:

      ![{C.CLOUD_LONG} new {C.VPC}](../assets/images/tiger-cloud-console-add-peering-vpc-tiger-console.webp)

   1.  Choose your region and IP range, name your VPC, then click `Create VPC`:

       ![Create a new VPC in {C.CLOUD_LONG}](../assets/images/tiger-cloud-console-configure-peering-vpc-tiger-console.webp)

       Your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and Peering {C.VPC} must be in the same AWS region. The number of Peering {C.VPC}s you can create in your project depends on your [{C.PRICING_PLAN}](/about/pricing-and-account-management/). If you need another Peering {C.VPC}, either contact [support@tigerdata.com](mailto:support@tigerdata.com) or change your plan in [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.timescale.com/).

   1.  Add a peering connection:

       1. In the `VPC Peering` column, click `Add`.
       1. Provide your AWS account ID, Transit Gateway ID, CIDR ranges, and AWS region. {C.CLOUD_LONG} creates a new isolated connection for every unique Transit Gateway ID. 
       
         ![Add peering](../assets/images/tiger-cloud-console-add-peering-tiger-console.webp)
 
       1. Click `Add connection`.

1. **Accept and configure peering connection in your AWS account**

   Once your peering connection appears as `Processing`, you can accept and configure it in AWS:

   1. Accept the peering request coming from {C.CLOUD_LONG}. The request can take up to 5 min to arrive. Within 5 more minutes after accepting, the peering should appear as `Connected` in {C.CONSOLE}.

   1. Configure at least the following in your AWS account networking:

      - Your subnet route table to route traffic to your Transit Gateway for the Peering VPC CIDRs.
      - Your Transit Gateway route table to route traffic to the newly created Transit Gateway peering attachment for the Peering VPC CIDRs.
      - Security groups to allow outbound TCP 5432.

1. **Attach a {C.CLOUD_LONG} service to the Peering VPC In [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.timescale.com/dashboard/services)**

   1. Select the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} you want to connect to the Peering VPC.
   1. Click `Operations` > `Security` > `VPC`.
   1. Select the VPC, then click `Attach VPC`.

   You cannot attach a {C.SERVICE_LONG} to multiple {C.CLOUD_LONG} {C.VPC}s at the same time.
