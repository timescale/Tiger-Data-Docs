### Attach a data exporter to a {C.SERVICE_LONG}

To send telemetry data to an external monitoring tool, you attach a data exporter to your
{C.SERVICE_LONG}. You can attach only one exporter to a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.

To attach an exporter: 

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, choose the ${C.SERVICE_SHORT}`}></NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Click `Operations` > `Exporters`"></NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Select the exporter, then click `Attach exporter`"></NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`If you are attaching a first \`Logs\` data type exporter, restart the ${C.SERVICE_SHORT}`}></NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

You can now monitor your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} metrics.

### Delete a data exporter

To remove a data exporter that you no longer need:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Disconnect the data exporter from your ${C.SERVICE_LONG}`}>

    1. In [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/services), choose the {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
    1. Click `Operations` > `Exporters`.
    1. Click the trash can icon next to the exporter.
    1. Repeat for every {C.SERVICE_SHORT} attached to the exporter you want to remove.

    The data exporter is now unattached from all {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s. However, it still exists in your project.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Delete the exporter on the project level">

    1. In {C.CONSOLE}, open [Exporters](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/integrations)
    1. Next to the exporter you want to edit, click menu > `Delete`
    1. Confirm that you want to delete the data exporter.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>
