With {C.CLOUD_LONG}, you are not limited to pre-set compute and storage. Get as much as you need when
provisioning your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s or later, as your needs grow.

* **Compute**: pay only for the compute resources you run. Compute is metered on an hourly
  basis, and you can [scale it up to 64 CPU](service-management/change-resources) at any time. You can also [scale out using replicas](high-availability/read-scaling)
  as your application grows. We also provide services to help you lower your compute needs
  while improving query performance. {C.CLOUD_LONG} is very efficient and generally needs less compute than other databases to deliver
  the same performance. The best way to size your needs is to sign up for a $1000 credit, 30-day free trial and test
  with a realistic workload.

* **Storage**: pay only for the storage you consume. You have high-performance storage for more frequently accessed data, and
  [low-cost bottomless blob storage](/build/data-management/storage) for older or less frequently accessed data. The high-performance storage offers you up to 64 TB of compressed
  (typically 80-100 TB uncompressed) data and is metered on your average GB consumption per hour. We can help you compress your data by up to 98% so you pay even less. <TieredStorageBilling />

For easy upgrades, each {C.SERVICE_SHORT} stores the {C.TIMESCALE_DB} binaries. This contributes up to 900 MB to overall storage, which amounts to less than $.80/month in additional storage costs.
