import * as C from "@constants";
import { Callout } from "@stainless-api/docs/components";
import { NumberedList, NumberedItem } from "@components/NumberedList";

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, click \`New service\`.`}>

This opens a three-step wizard.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Choose your compute and region, then click `Continue`.">

`Shared` is the free compute option. Under `Provider and region`, pick the `AWS` or `Azure` tab and
a region; see [supported regions](/get-started/supported-regions).

To create a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} without {C.TIMESCALE_DB} and the AI extensions, click
`Create with plain PostgreSQL`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Choose `Development` or `Production`, then click `Continue`.">

This is more than a label. Production {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s have delete protection and development ones
do not, and the two are upgraded on different schedules: a new minor version reaches `#dev`
{C.SERVICE_SHORT}s in the next maintenance window, and `#prod` {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s three weeks later,
so you can validate the release on development first. See
[Change the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} environment](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/service-management/service-management#change-the-service-environment)
and [Upgrades](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/upgrades#non-critical-upgrades).

You can also add high-availability replicas, a connection pooler, {C.VPC} peering, and private
endpoints. All of these can be changed later, including the environment.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Name your service, then click `Create service`.">

`Configuration summary` shows what you chose. To change something, click `Back`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`Under \`Download your database config\`, click \`Download the config\` and store the configuration information you need to connect to this ${C.SERVICE_SHORT} in a secure location.`}>

This file contains the passwords and configuration information you need to connect to your
{C.SERVICE_SHORT} from the command line, or using third-party database administration tools.

<Callout variant="important">

This is the only time you can access your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}'s password.

</Callout>

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

You can [manage, pause, or delete](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/service-management/service-management) your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} at any time from {C.CONSOLE}.
