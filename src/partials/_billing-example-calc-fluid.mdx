For example, over the last month your {C.SERVICE_LONG} has been running compute for 500 hours and 30 minutes:

- 375 hours and 30 minutes with 2 CPU
- 125 hours with 4 CPU

and consumed high-performance storage for 720 hours and 15 minutes:

- 200 hours and 15 minutes with 100 GB
- 520 hours with 150 GB

On a Standard {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, a started hour is billed in full:

**Compute cost** = (`376` x `hourly price for 2 CPU`) + (`125` x `hourly price for 4 CPU`)

**High-performance storage cost** = (`201` x `100 GB` x `hourly price per GB`) + (`520` x `150 GB` x `hourly price per GB`)

On a Fluid {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, compute and storage are billed by the minute:

**Compute cost** = (`375.5` x `hourly price for 2 CPU`) + (`125` x `hourly price for 4 CPU`)

**High-performance storage cost** = (`200.25` x `100 GB` x `hourly price per GB`) + (`520` x `150 GB` x `hourly price per GB`)
