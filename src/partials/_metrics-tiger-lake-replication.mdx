import * as C from "@constants";

These metrics track the replication slot that the {C.ICEBERG_CONNECTOR_LONG} uses to read changes from your service. These metrics are specific to the `timescaledb_lake_slot` replication slot only. Every metric carries the `project_id`, `region`, and `service_id` labels.
|Metric|Description|
|-|-|
|`tigerlake_replication_slot_lag_in_bytes`|Replication lag of the {C.ICEBERG_CONNECTOR_SHORT} slot, in bytes.|
|`tigerlake_replication_slot_flush_lag_in_bytes`|Flush lag of the {C.ICEBERG_CONNECTOR_SHORT} slot, in bytes.|
