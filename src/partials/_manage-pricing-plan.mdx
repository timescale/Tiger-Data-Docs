You handle all details about your {C.CLOUD_LONG} project including updates to your {C.PRICING_PLAN},
payment methods, and add-ons in the [billing section in {C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/billing/details):

<ThemeImage light={imgTigerCloudBillingLight} dark={imgTigerCloudBillingDark} alt="Adding a payment method in Timescale" />

- **Details**: an overview of your {C.PRICING_PLAN}, usage, and payment details. You can add up
  to three credit cards to your `Wallet`. If you prefer to pay by invoice,
  [contact {C.COMPANY}](https://www.tigerdata.com/contact/) and ask to change to corporate billing. You can also request early access to paying with crypto.

  The **Spend** section at the top of the Details page gives you a snapshot of your current billing period costs:

  - **Spend to date**: the amount accrued so far in the current billing period.
  - **Projected month-end**: an estimate of your total bill at the end of the month based on current usage.
  - **By product**: spend broken down by usage type: compute, storage, and add-ons.
  - **By service**: spend grouped by {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, showing each service's name and ID with its product-level costs (compute, storage, and add-ons such as connection poolers and VPCs) listed beneath it.

- **Timeline**: a chart of your spend over the last seven days, broken down by usage type (compute, storage, and add-ons). Use the Timeline to spot spending trends and identify which usage category is driving cost changes.

- **Emails**: the addresses {C.COMPANY} uses to communicate with you. Payment
  confirmations and alerts are sent to the email address you signed up with.
  Add another address to send details to other departments in your organization.

- **History**: the list of your downloadable {C.CLOUD_LONG} invoices.

- **Plans**: choose the {C.PRICING_PLAN} supplying the [features](pricing-and-account-management#features-included-in-each-pricing-plan) that suit your business and
  engineering needs.

- **Add-ons**: add `Production support` and improved database performance for mission-critical workloads.
