PITR forks use the [`timestamp` forking strategy](../service-management/fork-services#understand-service-forks). To recover from a destructive or unwanted action, create a fork at a specific point in time. To avoid paying compute for both the recovery fork and the original {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, pause the original.
