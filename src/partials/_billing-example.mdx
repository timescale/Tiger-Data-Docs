You are billed at the end of each month in arrears. Your monthly invoice
includes an itemized cost accounting for each {C.SERVICE_LONG} and any additional charges.

{C.CLOUD_LONG} charges are based on consumption and your pricing plan:

- **Compute**: {props.fluid ? <>prices are per hour. Standard services are billed by the hour, so you pay for a full hour even if the actual consumption is less. You can scale compute up and down at any time.</> : <>billed and metered on an hourly basis. This means that you are billed for a full hour even if the actual consumption is less. - **Storage**: {props.fluid ? <>priced per GB per hour. Standard services are metered by the hour, Fluid services by the minute.</> : <>metered on your average GB consumption per hour.</>} Storage grows and shrinks automatically with your data.
