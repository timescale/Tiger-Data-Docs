## Dropping {C.CHUNK}s times out

When you drop a {C.CHUNK}, it requires an exclusive lock. If a {C.CHUNK} is being
accessed by another session, you cannot drop the {C.CHUNK} at the same time. If a
drop {C.CHUNK} operation can't get the lock on the {C.CHUNK}, then it times out and the
process fails. To resolve this problem, check what is locking the {C.CHUNK}. In some
cases, this could be caused by a {C.CAGG} or other process accessing
the {C.CHUNK}. When the drop {C.CHUNK} operation can get an exclusive lock on the {C.CHUNK},
it completes as expected.

For more information about locks, see the
[{C.PG} lock monitoring documentation](https://wiki.postgresql.org/wiki/Lock_Monitoring).
