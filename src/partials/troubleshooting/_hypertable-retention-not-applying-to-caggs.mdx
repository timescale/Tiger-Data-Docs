## {C.HYPERTABLE_CAP} retention policy isn't applying to {C.CAGG}s

A retention policy set on a {C.HYPERTABLE} does not apply to any {C.CAGG}s
made from the {C.HYPERTABLE}. This allows you to set different retention
periods for raw and summarized data. To apply a retention policy to a {C.CAGG},
set the policy on the {C.CAGG} itself.
