## Service is running low on disk, memory, or CPU

**Message:**

```
Your Managed Service for TimescaleDB service, in project "ExampleAccount", is running low on
CPU. Running low on CPU affects performance and could affect service
availability. Please either optimize your usage pattern or reduce the workload,
and consider upgrading to a larger plan to avoid service outage.
```

When your database reaches 90% of your allocated disk, memory, or CPU resources,
an automated message with the text above is sent to your email address.

You can resolve this by logging in to your {C.MST_LONG}
account and changing your plan to increase your available resources:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Select the service">

From the {C.MST_LONG} Dashboard, select the {C.MST_SERVICE_SHORT} that you want to increase resources
for.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Change the plan">

Click `Service Settings` > `Service summary` > `Change Plan`. Select the suitable plan and click `Change Plan`.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

If you run out of resources regularly, you might need to consider using your
resources more efficiently. Consider enabling [hypercore](/build/columnar-storage/setup-hypercore),
using [continuous aggregates](/build/continuous-aggregates/create-a-continuous-aggregate),
or [configuring data retention](/learn/data-management/data-retention) to reduce the amount of
resources your database uses.
