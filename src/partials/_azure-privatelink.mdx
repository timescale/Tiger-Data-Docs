## Prerequisites

<PrereqsCloud />

* Create an [Azure account](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-gb/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account) with an active subscription.
* Configure [permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/private-link/rbac-permissions) to create private endpoints and manage network resources.
* Create an [Azure Virtual Network](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/virtual-network/quickstart-create-virtual-network?tabs=portal) with a subnet for the resources you will connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}.

## Set up Azure Private Link connection

<Callout variant="note">

Azure Private Link is currently in private preview. To request access in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints` and click `Request access`. Then refresh the page and follow the steps below.

</Callout>

Take the following steps to connect {C.CLOUD_LONG} to Azure with Private Link:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Create a Private Link subscription authorization">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/azure-privatelink), select `Security` > `Private Endpoints` > `Configure Private Endpoint Connection`.
1. Enter your [Azure subscription ID](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-portal/get-subscription-tenant-id#find-your-azure-subscription) and specify a name for the Private Link authorization. <Image src={imgConfigurePrivateLinkAuthorization} alt="Azure Private Link authorization" />

   <Callout variant="warning">

   Click the checkmark next to the authorization to save it. Otherwise, your input is discarded.

   </Callout>

1. Under `Alias`, copy the alias for the region in which you need to create the connection. Choose the region closest to your Azure resources for optimal performance.
1. Click `Done`. {C.CLOUD_LONG} confirms your authorization. Once it is confirmed, you can create multiple private endpoints from the same authorized subscription. <Image src={imgPrivateLinkAuthorizationComplete} alt="Azure Private Link authorization complete" />

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a private endpoint">

1. In [Azure Portal](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/get-started/azure-portal), go to `Private endpoints` and click `Create`. <Image src={imgCreatePrivateEndpointAzure} alt="Create Azure Private Endpoint" />
1. In `Subscription`, select the subscription you have previously authorized in {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
1. In `Resource group`, select an existing resource group or create a new one for your private endpoint.
1. Provide a name for your endpoint.
1. Select the region where your Virtual Network is deployed, then click `Next: Resource`.
1. In `Connection method`, select `Connect to an Azure resource by resource ID or alias`.
1. In `Resource ID or alias`, paste the alias you have copied from {C.CONSOLE_LONG}.
1. In `Request message`, enter your [{C.PROJECT_LONG} ID](/integrate/find-connection-details), then click `Next: Virtual Network`.
1. Select the Virtual Network and subnet for your endpoint, optionally select an application security group, then click `Next: DNS`.
1. Optionally configure private DNS integration and tags for your endpoint, then click `Next: Review + create`.
1. Review your config and click `Create`. Azure creates your private endpoint. Wait for the deployment to succeed.
1. Go to `Private endpoints` and copy the private endpoint IP from the `Private IP` column.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Sync the connection">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/azure-privatelink) > `Security` > `Private Link`, click `Refresh`. {C.CLOUD_LONG} automatically approves connections from authorized subscriptions. Your connection appears in the list. <Image src={imgPrivateLinkAddIp} alt="Azure Private Endpoint IP" />
1. Click `Add IP` and paste the IP address you have copied from Azure Portal.
1. Click the three dots next to your connection and select `+ Attach service`. Select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from the dropdown and click `Attach`. You can attach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to one Private Endpoint connection.
1. From a VM inside your Azure VNet, connect to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} using a connection string with your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details). You should be able to connect successfully.

<Callout variant="warning">

After attaching a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to a Private Endpoint, it is no longer accessible from the public internet. Make sure all your applications are configured to connect through the Private Endpoint before attaching your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.

</Callout>

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Manage connections

- To detach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from a Private Endpoint connection, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints` and expand the details of the corresponding connection. Click the trash bin icon and confirm detaching your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
- To edit or remove a connection, click the three dots next to the connection in the list and select `Edit` or `Disconnect`, respectively. Detach {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s from the connection before deleting it.
- To remove an authorization, click `Manage Authorizations` > trash bin icon. Disconnect all relevant connections before removing an authorization.
