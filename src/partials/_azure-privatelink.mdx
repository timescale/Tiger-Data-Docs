## Set up a private endpoint connection

Take the following steps to connect your {C.SERVICE_LONG} to a Private Link endpoint.

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Copy the alias for your region">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/azure-privatelink), select `Security` > `Private Endpoints` > `Manage endpoints`.
1. Under `Create the endpoint`, copy the `Alias` for the region in which you need to create the connection. Choose the region closest to your Azure resources for optimal performance.

   <Callout variant="warning">

   Screenshot pending: the `Manage endpoints` panel.

   </Callout>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a private endpoint in Azure">

1. In [Azure Portal](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/get-started/azure-portal), go to `Private endpoints` and click `Create`.

   <Image src={imgCreatePrivateEndpointAzure} alt="Create Azure Private Endpoint" />

1. In `Subscription`, select the subscription that contains the resources you want to connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
1. In `Resource group`, select an existing resource group or create a new one for your private endpoint.
1. Provide a name for your endpoint.
1. Select the region where your Virtual Network is deployed, then click `Next: Resource`.
1. In `Connection method`, select `Connect to an Azure resource by resource ID or alias`.
1. In `Resource ID or alias`, paste the alias you have copied from {C.CONSOLE_LONG}.
1. Optionally, in `Request message`, enter your [{C.PROJECT_LONG} ID](/integrate/find-connection-details). This can let {C.CLOUD_LONG} claim the connection for that project automatically. You claim the connection explicitly in a later step either way. Click `Next: Virtual Network`.
1. Select the Virtual Network that contains the resources you want to connect to {C.CLOUD_LONG}, then choose the subnet where the endpoint's private IP will be created. Optionally select an application security group, then click `Next: DNS`.
1. Optionally configure private DNS integration and tags for your endpoint, then click `Next: Review + create`.
1. Review your config and click `Create`. Azure creates your private endpoint. Wait for the deployment to succeed.
1. Go to your private endpoint's `Overview` and click `JSON View`. Under `properties`, copy the value of `resourceGuid`, in the form `f91412e6-92f8-40f0-b849-abe6ed04f61b`. You claim the connection with this value.

1. Go to `Private endpoints` and copy the private endpoint IP from the `Private IP` column.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Claim the connection">

1. In [{C.CONSOLE_LONG}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/azure-privatelink) > `Security` > `Private Endpoints`, click `Manage endpoints`.
1. Under `Claim it`, paste your `resourceGuid` into `Endpoint identifier`, then click `Claim connection`. {C.CLOUD_LONG} links the connection to the project you are working in, and it appears in the list.

   <Callout variant="warning">

   Screenshot pending: the `Claim it` section of the `Manage endpoints` panel.

   </Callout>

   <Callout variant="note">

   You can claim a private endpoint against one project only.

   Your `resourceGuid` can also take a minute to become claimable after you create the endpoint in Azure.

   </Callout>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Configure and attach">

1. Under `IP Address` click `Add IP` and paste the private endpoint IP address you have copied from Azure Portal.
1. Under `Services` click `Attach service`. Select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from the dropdown and click `Attach`. You can attach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to one private endpoint.
1. From a VM inside your Azure VNet, connect to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} using a connection string with your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details). You should be able to connect successfully.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Manage connections

- To detach a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from a private endpoint connection, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints`, expand the arrow in the `Services` column, and click the trash icon next to the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} connection string.
- To edit or remove an endpoint connection, go to `Security` > `Private Endpoints` and click the three dots next to the connection in the list. Select `Edit` or `Disconnect`, respectively. You need to detach all {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s from a private endpoint connection before deleting it.
