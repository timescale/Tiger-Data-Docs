A {C.SERVICE_LONG} is a single optimized {C.PG} instance extended with innovations in the database engine and cloud
infrastructure to deliver speed without sacrifice. A {C.SERVICE_LONG} is 10-1000x faster at scale! It
is ideal for applications requiring strong data consistency, complex relationships, and advanced querying capabilities.
Get ACID compliance, extensive SQL support, JSON handling, and extensibility through custom functions, data types, and
extensions.

Each {C.SERVICE_SHORT} is associated with a project in {C.CLOUD_LONG}. Each project can have multiple {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s. Each user is a [member of one or more projects](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/security/members).

You create and manage {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}, or from the terminal with {C.CLI_LONG} and {C.MCP_LONG}.

{C.CLOUD_LONG} offers three {C.SERVICE_SHORT} types:

- **Standard**: the proven choice for production workloads, hosting thousands of production databases today, with an uptime SLA and a choice of [cloud providers and regions](/get-started/supported-regions). Offers the widest compute range and the most networking and security options. Supports forks as complete, independent copies of a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} for staging and disaster recovery. Runs on the cloud provider's block storage.

- **Fluid** (public preview): the choice for development, CI, and agent workflows on production-scale data, and for I/O-heavy workloads. On top of regular full-copy forks, it supports branches: lightweight copies of a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} that are ready in a minute or two, whatever the data size. A branch shares the primary {C.SERVICE_SHORT}'s storage and only writes the data that changes, so it costs a fraction of a fork. Queries and ingest are faster on the same compute. Runs on Fluid storage, a distributed storage layer built by {C.COMPANY}.{props.azure && <> Not available on Azure yet.</>}

- **Free**: a limited Standard {C.SERVICE_SHORT} at zero cost. The choice for schema exploration, syntax checks, and other small-scale testing. Runs on shared compute with 1 GiB of storage, on AWS `us-east-1` only, as a development environment with a basic feature scope. Once you reach the storage limit, the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} becomes read-only. You can [convert it to Standard](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/service-management/service-management#convert-a-free-service-to-a-standard-one) at any time.

Standard and Fluid {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s share the full {C.CLOUD_LONG} toolkit: [{C.HYPERTABLE}s](/learn/hypertables/understand-hypertables) and [{C.CAGG}s](/build/continuous-aggregates/create-a-continuous-aggregate) for time-series data at scale, the [{C.COLUMNSTORE}](/reference/timescaledb/hypercore) and [tiered storage](/build/data-management/storage/manage-storage) to keep costs down as data ages, full-text and vector search, [automatic backups and point-in-time recovery](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/high-availability/backup-restore), [high availability](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/high-availability/high-availability), [{C.READ_REPLICA}s](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/high-availability/read-scaling), [connection pooling](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/service-management/connection-pooling), and [monitoring](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/monitoring) with metrics and insights.

You can [manage, pause, or delete](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/service-management/service-management) your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} at any time from {C.CONSOLE}, {C.CLI_LONG}, or {C.MCP_LONG}.
