When you sign up for a [$1000-credit, 30-day free trial](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/), {C.CLOUD_LONG} creates a {C.PROJECT_SHORT} with built-in role-based access. A single email address can create only one {C.PROJECT_SHORT}. To work across multiple {C.PROJECT_SHORT}s, ask an Owner to add you to their {C.PROJECT_SHORT}.

Role-based access includes the following roles:

- **Owner**: {C.CLOUD_LONG} assigns this role to you when your {C.PROJECT_SHORT} is created. As the Owner, you can add and delete other users, transfer project ownership, administer {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s, and edit {C.PROJECT_SHORT} settings.
- **Admin**: the Owner assigns this role to other users in the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}. A user with the Admin role has the same scope of rights as the Owner but cannot transfer project ownership.
- **Developer**: the Owner and Admins assign this role to other users in the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}. A Developer can build, deploy, and operate {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s across projects, but does not have administrative privileges over users, roles, or billing. A Developer can invite other users to the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}, but only with the Viewer role.
- **Viewer**: the Owner and Admins assign this role to other users in the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}. A Viewer has limited, read-only access to {C.CONSOLE_LONG}. This means that a Viewer cannot modify {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s and their configurations in any way. A Viewer has no access to {C.DATA_MODE} and has read-queries-only access to {C.SQL_EDITOR}.

<Image src={imgTigerConsoleProjectRolesOverview} alt="Project users in Console" />

If you have the [{C.ENTERPRISE} {C.PRICING_PLAN}](../pricing-and-account-management), you can use your company [SAML](../security/saml)
identity provider to log in to {C.CONSOLE_SHORT}.

<Callout variant="note">

User roles in a {C.PROJECT_LONG} do not overlap with the database-level roles for the individual {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s. This page describes the {C.PROJECT_SHORT} roles available in {C.CONSOLE_SHORT}. For the database-level user roles, see [Manage data security in your {C.SERVICE_LONG}](../security/read-only-role).

</Callout>

New users do not need to have a {C.ACCOUNT_LONG} before you add them, they are
prompted to create one when they respond to the confirmation email. Existing users
join a {C.PROJECT_SHORT} in addition to the other {C.PROJECT_SHORT}s they are already members of.

## Transfer {C.PROJECT_SHORT} ownership

Each {C.PROJECT_LONG} has one Owner. As the {C.PROJECT_SHORT} Owner, you have rights to
add and delete users, edit {C.PROJECT_SHORT} settings, and transfer the Owner role to another user. When you transfer
ownership to another user, you lose your ownership rights.

To transfer {C.PROJECT_SHORT} ownership:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, click \`Users\``}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Next to the person you want to transfer project ownership to, click `⋮` > `Transfer project ownership`">

If you are unable to transfer ownership, hover over the greyed out button to see the details.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Enter your password, and click `Verify`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Complete the two-factor authentication challenge and click `Confirm`">

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

If you have the [{C.ENTERPRISE} {C.PRICING_PLAN}](../pricing-and-account-management), and log in to {C.CLOUD_LONG} using [SAML authentication](../security/saml)
or have not enabled [two-factor authentication](../security/multi-factor-authentication), [contact support](https://www.tigerdata.com/contact) to transfer
{C.PROJECT_SHORT} ownership.
