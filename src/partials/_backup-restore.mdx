{C.CLOUD_LONG} provides comprehensive backup and recovery solutions to protect your data, including automatic daily backups,
configurable retention, cross-region protection, and point-in-time recovery.

## Automatic backups

{C.CLOUD_LONG} automatically handles backup for your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s using the `pgBackRest` tool. You don't need to perform
backups manually. What's more, with [cross-region backup](#enable-cross-region-backup), you are protected when an entire region goes down.

{C.CLOUD_LONG} automatically creates one full backup every week, and incremental backups every day in the same region as
your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}. Additionally, all [Write-Ahead Log (WAL)](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/wal-intro.html) files are retained back to the oldest full backup.
This means that you always have a full backup available for the current and previous week:

<Image src={imgDatabaseBackupRecovery} alt="Database backup and recovery architecture in Tiger Cloud" />

On [{C.SCALE} and {C.ENTERPRISE}](../pricing-and-account-management) {C.PRICING_PLAN}s, you can check the list of backups for your retention period in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}. To do so, select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, then click `Operations` > `Backup and restore` > `Backup history`. On {C.ENTERPRISE}, you can [extend backup retention](#extend-backup-retention) up to 180 days.

In the event of a storage failure, a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} automatically recovers from a backup
to the point of failure. If the whole availability zone goes down, your {C.SERVICE_LONG}s are recovered in a different zone. In the event of a user error, you can [create a point-in-time recovery fork](#recover-to-a-point-in-time).

## Extend backup retention

<Callout variant="note">
Extended backup retention is available on the [{C.ENTERPRISE}](../pricing-and-account-management) {C.PRICING_PLAN}.
</Callout>

{C.CLOUD_LONG} keeps full backups, incremental backups, and WAL files for the retention period included with your
[{C.PRICING_PLAN}](../pricing-and-account-management#features-included-in-each-pricing-plan). This means that you can
recover your data to any point in that period. To meet longer compliance or audit requirements, on {C.ENTERPRISE} you
can set a retention period of up to 180 days for each {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.

Extended retention covers backups and WAL files in the same way as the default retention period. The recovery window for
[point-in-time recovery forks](#recover-to-a-point-in-time) matches your retention period: if you set retention
to 90 days, you can fork your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to any point in the previous 90 days. When you extend the retention
period, backups accumulate until they cover it in full; when you shorten it, the backups outside the new period are
deleted soon afterwards and are no longer available for recovery.

To change the retention period:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Select your ${C.SERVICE_SHORT} and open backup settings`}>

In [{C.CONSOLE_SHORT}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/services), select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and click `Operations` > `Backup and restore`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Set the retention period">

In `Backup retention`, select one of the preset periods, or click `Custom` and set a number of days between 14 and 180,
then confirm your choice.

<ThemeImage light={props.retentionScreenshotLight} dark={props.retentionScreenshotDark} alt="Setting the backup retention period in Tiger Console" />

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Enable cross-region backup

<Callout variant="note">
Cross-region backup is available on [{C.ENTERPRISE}](../pricing-and-account-management) pricing plan.
</Callout>

For added reliability, you can enable cross-region backup. This protects your data when an entire region goes down. In this case, you have two identical backups of your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} at any time, but one of them is in a different region. Cross-region backups are updated daily and weekly in the same way as a regular backup. You can have one cross-region backup for a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.

Cross-region backups are always kept for 14 days. [Extended backup retention](#extend-backup-retention) applies only to
the backups in the region your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} runs in.

You enable cross-region backup when you create a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, or configure it for an existing {C.SERVICE_SHORT} in {C.CONSOLE_LONG}:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Select your ${C.SERVICE_SHORT} and open backup settings`}>

In [{C.CONSOLE_SHORT}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/services), select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and click `Operations` > `Backup and restore`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Enable cross-region backup">

In `Backup regions`, select the region in the dropdown and click `Enable backup`.

<ThemeImage light={props.createBackupScreenshotLight} dark={props.createBackupScreenshotDark} alt="Creating a cross-region backup in Tiger Console" />

You can now see the backup, its region, and creation date in a list.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

You can have one cross-region backup per {C.SERVICE_SHORT}. To change the region of your backup:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Open backup settings for your ${C.SERVICE_SHORT}`}>

In [{C.CONSOLE_SHORT}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/dashboard/services), select your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and click `Operations` > `Backup and restore`.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Disable the existing backup">

Click the trash icon next to the existing backup to disable it.

<ThemeImage light={props.backupListScreenshotLight} dark={props.backupListScreenshotDark} alt="Cross-region backup list in Tiger Console" />

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Create a new backup in a different region">

In `Backup regions`, select the new region in the dropdown and click `Enable backup`.

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Recover to a point in time

When data is lost or damaged, by an accidental delete or a bad migration for example, you recover it with a
point-in-time recovery (PITR) fork: a copy of your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} as it was at a moment before the problem started.
You then point your app at the fork. The original {C.SERVICE_SHORT} stays untouched.
