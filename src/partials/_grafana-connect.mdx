## Connect Grafana to {C.CLOUD_LONG}

To visualize the results of your queries, enable Grafana to read the data in your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title="Log in to Grafana">

In your browser, log in to either:
 - Self-hosted Grafana: at `http://localhost:3000/`. The default credentials are `admin`, `admin`.
 - Grafana Cloud: use the URL and credentials you set when you created your account.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`Add your ${C.SERVICE_SHORT} as a data source`}>

<ol type="a">
<li>Open `Connections` > `Data sources`, then click `Add new data source`.</li>
<li>Select `{C.PG}` from the list.</li>
<li>

Configure the connection:
   - `Host URL`, `Database name`, `Username`, and `Password`, configure using your [connection details](/integrate/find-connection-details). `Host URL` is in the format `<host>:<port>`.
   - `TLS/SSL Mode`: select `require`.
   - `{C.PG} options`: enable `TimescaleDB`.
   - Leave the default setting for all other fields.

</li>
<li>

Click `Save & test`.

Grafana checks that your details are set correctly.

</li>
</ol>

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>
