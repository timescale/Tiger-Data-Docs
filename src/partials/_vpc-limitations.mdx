import * as C from "@constants";

* You **can attach**:
  * Up to 50 Customer {C.VPC}s to a Peering {C.VPC}.
  * A {C.SERVICE_LONG} to a single Peering {C.VPC} at a time.
   The {C.SERVICE_SHORT} and the Peering {C.VPC} must be in the same AWS region. However, you can peer a Customer {C.VPC} and a Peering {C.VPC} that are in different regions.
  * Multiple {C.SERVICE_LONG}s to the same Peering {C.VPC}.
* You **cannot attach** a {C.SERVICE_LONG} to multiple Peering {C.VPC}s at the same time.

  The number of Peering {C.VPC}s you can create in your project depends on your [{C.PRICING_PLAN}](/deploy/tiger-cloud/tiger-cloud-aws/pricing-and-account-management).
  If you need another Peering {C.VPC}, either contact [support@tigerdata.com](mailto:support@tigerdata.com) or change your {C.PRICING_PLAN} in [{C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/).
