When you sign up for {C.CLOUD_LONG}, a {C.PROJECT_SHORT} is created for you automatically, and you are its Owner. A single email address can create only one {C.PROJECT_SHORT} this way. To work across multiple {C.PROJECT_SHORT}s, ask an Owner to add you to their {C.PROJECT_SHORT}.

## Delete a {C.PROJECT_SHORT}

The Owner can delete a {C.PROJECT_SHORT} and everything left in it: the exporters, VPCs, private endpoints, credentials, API keys, activity history, and metrics. This cannot be undone.

{C.SERVICE_SHORT_CAP}s are not deleted with the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}. Delete them first, starting with any read replicas.

You can only delete a {C.PROJECT_SHORT} from {C.CONSOLE_SHORT} if you are its only Owner and you have another {C.PROJECT_SHORT}. Otherwise, `Settings` explains the reason instead of offering the delete action:

- More than one Owner: transfer or change the other Owner roles first, or [open a support request](https://www.tigerdata.com/contact).
- Your last {C.PROJECT_SHORT}: to remove everything, [delete your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT}](#delete-your-account) instead.

To delete a {C.PROJECT_SHORT}:

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, click \`Settings\``}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="In `Delete project`, click `Delete project`">

The confirmation lists what is removed along with the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}, what you still owe, and how many other members lose access. Those members are not notified.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Type the project name to confirm, then click `Delete project`">

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

If the {C.PROJECT_SHORT} has an unpaid invoice, or usage that has not been invoiced yet, the deletion is queued instead of running immediately. The {C.PROJECT_SHORT} is deleted automatically once the due invoice and the final usage invoice for the month are paid. Until then, `Services` shows a notice and no new {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s can be created in the {C.PROJECT_SHORT}, including through {C.CLI_LONG} and the API.

## Delete your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT}

Deleting your {C.ACCOUNT_LONG} removes your name, email address, and sign-in, together with every {C.PROJECT_SHORT} you own and the resources left in them. This cannot be undone.

{C.SERVICE_SHORT_CAP}s are not deleted with your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT}. Delete them first, starting with any read replicas.

To delete your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT}:

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, open the menu under your profile icon and click \`Account settings\``}>

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedItem>
