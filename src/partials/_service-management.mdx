From the `Operations` dashboard, you can reset the password, change the environment, convert a free {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to a standard one, pause, or delete the {C.SERVICE_SHORT}. To fork a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, see [Fork {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s](../service-management/fork-services).

## Reset your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} password

You can reset your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} password from the `Operations` dashboard. This is the
password you use to connect to your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, not the password for {C.CONSOLE}. To reset your {C.CONSOLE_SHORT} password, navigate to the `Account` page.

When you reset your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} password, you are prompted for your {C.CONSOLE_SHORT} password. When you have authenticated, you can create a new {C.SERVICE_SHORT} password,
ask {C.CONSOLE_SHORT} to auto-generate a password, or switch your authentication
type between SCRAM and MD5.

SCRAM (salted challenge response authentication mechanism) and MD5 (message
digest algorithm 5) are cryptographic authentication mechanisms. {C.CONSOLE_LONG}
uses SCRAM by default. It is more secure and strongly recommended. The MD5
option is provided for compatibility with older clients.

<Callout variant="warning">
Resetting the password immediately invalidates the old one. Any application, automation, or integration that connects with the previous credentials loses access until you update its connection details. Update every connection string that uses this {C.SERVICE_SHORT} before or right after you reset the password.
</Callout>

## Change the {C.SERVICE_SHORT} environment

In {C.CLOUD_LONG}, you create {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s for the following environments:

- Development {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s: for less demanding tasks, such as ad hoc analytics, testing, or application building. Development {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s have no delete protection.
- Production {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s: for mission-critical applications like client-facing dashboards or APIs. Production {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s have delete protection.

{C.CLOUD_LONG} applies upgrades differently to development and production {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s.

## Pause a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}

You can pause a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} if you want to stop it running temporarily. When you
pause a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, you are no longer billed for compute resources. However, you do
need to continue paying for any storage you are using. Pausing a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} ensures
that it is still available, and is ready to be restarted at any time.

## Delete a {C.SERVICE_SHORT}

You can delete a {C.SERVICE_SHORT} to remove it completely. This removes the {C.SERVICE_SHORT}
and its underlying data from the server. You cannot recover a deleted
{C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
