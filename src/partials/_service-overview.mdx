import * as C from "@constants";

You use {C.CONSOLE_LONG} to manage your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s and data in a convenient, centralized manner. When you [log into {C.CONSOLE}](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/), you see `Services` and other {C.PROJECT_SHORT} management tabs:

![Check {C.SERVICE_LONG} is running](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-on-azure/tiger-console-services-view.png)

- `Services`: create a new [{C.SERVICE_SHORT}](/get-started/create-service) in your {C.PROJECT_SHORT}, and view the existing {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s and their statuses. Each {C.SERVICE_SHORT} is an optimized [{C.PG}](/learn/glossary#postgresql) instance extended with database engine innovations such as [{C.TIMESCALE_DB}](/learn/glossary#timescaledb), managed for you by {C.CLOUD_LONG}.
- `Security`: create [{C.VPC}s](../../security) and [IP allowlists](../../security).
- `Exporters`: [export metrics and logs](../../monitoring) from your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s.
- `Activity`: [check your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s](/deploy/tiger-cloud/understand/changelog#activity-log) and {C.PROJECT_SHORT} activity.
- `Settings`: [create client credentials](/integrate/find-connection-details#create-client-credentials), add AI model API keys, and request a SOC 2 report.
- `CLI/MCP`: install {C.CLI_LONG} and set up {C.MCP_LONG}.
- `Users`: [add and remove users](/deploy/tiger-cloud/secure-access/configure-role-based-access) in your {C.PROJECT_SHORT}.
- `Billing`: [check usage](/deploy/tiger-cloud/understand/pricing-and-account-management#monitor-usage-and-costs), [change {C.PRICING_PLAN}s](/deploy/tiger-cloud/understand/pricing-and-account-management#upgrade-or-downgrade-your-pricing-plans-at-any-time), and manage payment methods.
- `Support`: open and manage [support tickets](/deploy/tiger-cloud/understand/pricing-and-account-management#tiger-cloud-support).

When you select a {C.SERVICE_LONG} in the `Services` tab, you land in the {C.OPS_MODE}. In this view, you manage your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}s. You see `Overview` and other related tabs:

![Select a service to edit](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-on-azure/ops-mode-overview-tiger-console.png)

- `Overview`: get an overview of your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} details, infrastructure, [connection info](/get-started/create-service#connect-to-your-service), and performance optimization tips.
- `Actions`: [connect and import data](/migrate) into your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
- `Explorer`: [inspect objects and storage configuration](../service-explorer) for your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.
- `Monitoring`: [get visibility](../../monitoring) into your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} performance.
- `Connectors`: [sync or stream](/migrate) directly in your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, so data from another source is continuously updated.
- `Operations`: fully manage your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}: [configure compute and storage](../change-resources), [set up replicas](../../high-availability), [back up](../../data-security), upgrade, and more.
- `AI`: create vector embeddings and [manage AI extensions](../../tiger-cloud-extensions) for your {C.SERVICE_SHORT}.

Use the toggle at the top to switch to {C.DATA_MODE}. In this view, you can [write queries with autocomplete](/get-started/run-queries-from-console#data-view) for any {C.SERVICE_SHORT}, save them in folders, share them, and create charts/dashboards with the added power of AI.

To query your {C.SERVICE_SHORT} from any tab, click `SQL Editor` at the bottom. The floating window expands and is ready for work. Click the window icon in its top right to change the editor position on the screen.
