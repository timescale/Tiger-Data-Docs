You can use two-factor authentication to log in to your {C.ACCOUNT_LONG}. Two-factor authentication, also known as two-step verification or 2FA, enables
secure logins that require an authentication code in addition to your user
password. The code is provided by an authenticator app on your mobile device. There are multiple authenticator apps available.

![{C.CONSOLE_LONG} 2FA](https://assets.timescale.com/docs/images/tiger-cloud-console/tiger-console-2fa.png)

This page describes how to configure two-factor authentication with Google Authenticator.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

*   Installed the [Google Authenticator application](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1066447)
    on your mobile device.

## Configure two-factor authentication with Google Authenticator

Take the following steps to configure two-factor authentication:

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Log in to ${C.CONSOLE} with your username and password. 2FA is not available if you log in with Google SSO.`}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`Click your user name in the top right of ${C.CONSOLE} and select \`Account settings\``}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Click `Add two-factor authentication`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="On your mobile device, open Google Authenticator, tap `+`, and select `Scan a QR code`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`Scan the QR code provided by ${C.CONSOLE} in \`Connect to an authenticator app\` and click \`Next\``}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`In ${C.CONSOLE}, enter the verification code provided by Google Authenticator, and click \`Next\``}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="In `Save your recovery codes`, copy, download, or print the recovery codes">

These are used to recover your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT} if you lose your device.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Verify that you have saved your recovery codes, by clicking `OK, I saved my recovery codes`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="If two-factor authentication is enabled correctly, an email notification is sent to you">

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

<Callout variant="info">

If you lose access to the mobile device you use for multi-factor authentication,
and you do not have access to your recovery codes, you cannot sign in to your
{C.ACCOUNT_LONG}. To regain access to your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT},
contact [support@tigerdata.com](mailto:support@tigerdata.com).

</Callout>

## Regenerate recovery codes

If you do not have access to your authenticator app and need to log in to
{C.CONSOLE}, you can use your recovery codes. Recovery codes are single-use. If you've used all 10
recovery codes, or lost access to them, you can generate another list. Generating a new list invalidates all previously generated codes.

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Log in to ${C.CONSOLE} with your username and password`}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Click your user name in the top right and select `Account settings`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="In `Two-factor authentication`, click `Regenerate recovery codes`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Enter the verification code from your authenticator app">

Alternatively, if you do not have access to the authenticator app,
click `Use recovery code instead` to enter a recovery code.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Click `Next`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="In `Save your recovery codes`, copy, download, or print the recovery codes">

These are used to recover your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT} if you lose your device.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Verify that you have saved your recovery codes, by clicking `OK, I saved my recovery codes`">

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>

## Remove two-factor authentication

If you need to enroll a new device for two-factor authentication, you can
remove two-factor authentication from your {C.ACCOUNT_SHORT} and then add it
again with your new device.

<NumberedList>

<NumberedItem title={`Log in to ${C.CONSOLE} with your username and password`}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title={`Click your user name in the top right of ${C.CONSOLE} and select \`Account settings\``}>

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="In `Two-factor authentication`, click `Remove two-factor authentication`">

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Enter the verification code from your authenticator app to confirm">

Alternatively click `Use recovery code instead` to type the recovery code.

</NumberedItem>

<NumberedItem title="Click `Remove`">

</NumberedItem>

</NumberedList>
