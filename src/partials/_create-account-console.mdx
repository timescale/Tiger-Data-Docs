To set up {C.CLOUD_LONG}:

<Steps>

<Step title="Sign up for a 30-day free trial">

Open [Sign up for {C.CLOUD_LONG}][timescale-signup] and add your details, then click `Start your free trial`. You receive a confirmation email in your inbox.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm your email address">

In the confirmation email, click the link supplied.

</Step>

<Step title="Select the pricing plan">

You are now logged into {C.CONSOLE_LONG}. You can change the pricing plan to better accommodate your growing needs on the [`Billing` page][console-billing].

</Step>

</Steps>

[console-billing]: https://console.cloud.timescale.com/dashboard/billing/plans
[pricing-plans]: /about/pricing-and-account-management/
[timescale-signup]: https://console.cloud.timescale.com/signup
